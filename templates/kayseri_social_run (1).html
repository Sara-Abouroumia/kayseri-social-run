01 Our Philosophy

More Than
Miles.

We're not just a running club. We're a movement born in the streets and trails of Kayseri — fuelled by youth energy, the shadow of Erciyes, and the belief that sport builds community.

Every Saturday morning, we lace up together. Every finish line, we cross together. No pace requirements, no ego — just people who believe in showing up.
